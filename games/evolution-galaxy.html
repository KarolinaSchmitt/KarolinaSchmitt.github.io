Overview

Evolution Galaxy is a merge and collection game where players combine species to evolve new life forms and expand across planets, spending several resources to speed up progress.

I design and balance the systems that gate merging, unlocking, and speed-ups, and the sinks (planet unlocks, deity interactions, accelerators) that keep progression from collapsing as players get stronger. I model how quickly collections fill for new and long-term players and re-pace the curve whenever new content ships, keeping the merge loop the thing players are actually engaging with.
