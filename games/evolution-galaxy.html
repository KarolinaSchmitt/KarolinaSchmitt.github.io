Evolution Galaxy: Mutant Merge

Mobile · Merge / idle / collection

Evolution Galaxy: Mutant Merge is a free-to-play merge and idle game that takes the evolution series into space. Players drag and combine creatures to evolve new mutants, then use their eggs and quick taps to earn the coins that unlock more species. Each planet brings its own theme, gods to discover and impostors, all in a hand-drawn doodle style.

I joined Evolution Galaxy when it was already live, as the only game designer in a five-person team. As an older game, its main fight was against the natural drop in store relevance.

The team's goal was to maximize the game's metrics with as few resources as possible, running data-driven A/B tests and moving monetization from ads to in-app purchases (IAP).

My contributions

  • A/B testingI ran data-driven A/B tests on the price and composition of offers, and on how often and when they appeared.
  • Monetization designI took part in moving the game's monetization from ads to in-app purchases (IAP).
  • Economy & balancingI balanced the game's IAP store and created new offers.
  • Promotional contentI created a system for promotional content that was easy to optimize and reuse.
  • Balancing spreadsheetsI maintained the spreadsheets used to balance the game.

Challenges & solutions

Staying relevant with an aging game

In an older game, store visibility falls naturally over time, and the team was small. The challenge was keeping the game relevant with limited resources. The focus was on:

  • Small, high-impact changesPrioritizing the changes that could move the metrics at the lowest cost to the team.
  • Reusable systemsBuilding systems that could be optimized and reused instead of rebuilt for every campaign.

Moving from ads to in-app purchases

Changing how a game makes money affects the whole experience. The challenge was growing IAP revenue as monetization moved away from ads. The focus was on:

  • A balanced storeBalancing the IAP store and creating new offers.
  • Offers at the right timeTesting the price and composition of offers, and how often and when they appear.

Deciding with data when resources are limited

With a small team, every change had to be worth making. Decisions were guided by data, in a cycle of:

  • TestComparing versions of an offer or of its timing in A/B tests.
  • MeasureReading the metrics of each version before choosing which to keep.
  • ReuseTurning what worked into a system that could be applied again.

Results

Despite the natural decline in relevance of an older game, Evolution Galaxy's metrics stayed stable during the period of work on the project.

  • RevenueTotal revenue grew 20% in the period after a specific IAP offer launched.
  • Monetization mixIAP's share of revenue increased as monetization moved from ads to purchases.
  • Reusable promotionsThe promotional content system was reused many times across campaigns.

In a mature game, relevance is earned with small, measured steps and tools that can be reused.
