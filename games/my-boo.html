My Boo

Mobile · Virtual pet / simulation

My Boo is a free-to-play virtual pet game in which players care for their Boo every day and play adventure minigames to earn coins. In the store, coins unlock outfits and accessories to customize the Boo, who can even dress up as a kitten, a dog and other animals.

I joined My Boo when it was already live, as the only game designer in a five-person team. With more than ten years in the stores, the main fight was against the natural depreciation that games face over time.

The team's goal was to keep the game stable with few resources, through special LiveOps events, tests and the economy behind them.

My contributions

  • Feature designI designed a collection feature.
  • Economy & balancingI adjusted the game's economy in step with the LiveOps events.
  • LiveOps designI planned a calendar of special LiveOps events.
  • Monetization designI ran tests with ads and planned events designed to encourage use of the store.
  • A/B testingI ran tests on the events calendar.
  • Promotional contentI produced promotional content on the same tooling base as Evolution Galaxy.

Challenges & solutions

Keeping a long-running game stable

Over the years, store visibility and engagement fall naturally. The challenge was keeping the game stable with a small team. The focus was on:

  • A calendar of eventsPlanning special LiveOps events to keep players engaged throughout the year.
  • New things to doDesigning a collection feature.

Connecting events, economy and store

A special event only works if the economy and the store support it. The challenge was making these three parts work together. The focus was on:

  • Economy in step with eventsAdjusting the economy together with the LiveOps events.
  • Events that bring players to the storePlanning events designed to encourage use of the store.

Deciding with tests when resources are limited

With few resources, each change had to be tested before being kept. The tests covered:

  • AdsRunning tests with ads.
  • Events calendarTesting how the events calendar was organized.

Results

During the period of work on the project, My Boo, a game with more than ten years in the stores, stayed stable despite the natural depreciation that games face over time.

  • A planned calendarThe game gained a calendar of special LiveOps events, aligned with the economy and the store.
  • Decisions backed by testsTests on ads and on the events calendar guided the team's decisions.

In a long-running game, stability is built with a steady calendar and constant testing.
