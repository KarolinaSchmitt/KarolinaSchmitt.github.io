Overview

My Boo is a long-running virtual-pet game where players feed, clean, play with, and customize their Boo, with mini-games, daily challenges, and seasonal events.

I design the daily reward cycle that brings players back, pace the customization catalogue so there is always an aspirational next goal, and build seasonal and themed events into the LiveOps calendar. Mini-games and challenges are balanced as reward sources against the customization and upgrade sinks.
