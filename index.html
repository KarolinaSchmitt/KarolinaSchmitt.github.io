Selected work

Live F2P · Tapps Games

Pet Shop Fever

Time management · Systems, economy, LiveOps

A live time-management game where players run and grow a chain of pet shops across several worlds, each with its own currencies and progression. I own the economy across those worlds, mapping soft and hard currency sources against their sinks so players never enter a new world in deficit, tune difficulty so recurring purchases have a clear role, and shape the store and contextual offers to lift average transaction value.

Multi-currency economy Source / sink balancing Booster economy Offer design A/B testing Weekly LiveOps

Evolution Galaxy: Mutant Merge

Merge / idle / collection · Systems, economy

A merge and collection game where players combine species to evolve new life forms and expand across planets. I balance the currencies and resources that gate merging, unlocking and speed-ups, and design the sinks that keep the economy from inflating as players progress. I model how quickly collections fill for new and long-term players and adjust the curve whenever new content ships.

Merge economy Resource sinks Progression pacing Monetization

Vlogger Go Viral

Idle / simulation · Systems, economy

An idle game about growing a video channel from a bedroom setup to an automated media operation. I tune the exponential curves behind views, income, upgrade costs and prestige so growth feels fast early and still means something a hundred hours in, pace content unlocks around short sessions and offline earnings, and balance IAP and ad-based boosts against that curve.

Idle curve tuning Prestige Session design A/B testing

My Boo

Virtual pet / casual simulation · Systems, economy, LiveOps

A long-running virtual-pet game with a daily care loop, mini-games and seasonal events. I design the currency and reward cycle that brings players back each day, price and pace the customization catalogue so there is always an aspirational next purchase, and build seasonal and themed LiveOps events into the calendar.

Daily loop Customization economy Seasonal LiveOps Retention
